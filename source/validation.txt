v0.8: contact-rhythm 2D scratching preview.
14 logic groups and 12 production Canvas groups passed once after final integration; 572 scene samples, maximum canvas-edge alpha 0.
Inline decoded-image loading, RAF, play/pause, half speed, replay, paw-zoom crop and reduced-motion initialization checks passed. Individually frozen decoded frames preserve the intended contact sequence.
The prior single shared whole-body interpolation is replaced by independent near/far paw phases: hook hold, resisted short pull, pull pause, release away from the board, curved lift, and approach.
Near/far timings differ; shoulder and elbow loading is delayed, head response is small and opposite, hip counterbalance is small, and rear feet remain planted.
The unchanged 400px board PNG draws to canonical [120,0,280,400]. JS metadata and generated C# constants share that rectangle; WPF applies .6 scale and +8.4 y.
The active relative-path generator is bake_scratch_contact.cjs (Node with @napi-rs/canvas, only for optional asset rebuilding). From the extracted package directory run: node source/bake_scratch_contact.cjs --out-dir motion-build, then copy motion-build/scratch-contact-atlas.png to source/assets/scratch-smooth.png and motion-build/scratch-contact-layout.json to source/assets/scratch-fullbody-layout.json. Normal Windows startup uses the included PNG and does not require Node.
The historical v0.7 builder is retained for reproducing the older motion and must not regenerate active assets.
Visual inspection of adjacent PNGs confirms planted near-paw tips during the hold, a short pull followed by pause, and visible alpha clearance from both sisal and wood on return. The other paw is offset in phase.
The wrist/palm patch uses one rigid local transform on a connected source-B mesh. Candidate frames contain no flipped triangles or previously observed wrist tears.
Naturalness limitations remain: mostly fixed toe/claw shape, no true per-toe grip or visibility exchange, visible 2D wrist/palm motion, and modest shoulder/chest loading. Geometry and pixel diagnostics do not prove natural biological motion.
Four-image loading, shared timing, clipping, stationary-board compositing, and care/sleep/drag/random-play/save checks are recorded by test-canvas.cjs and test-logic.cjs after integration.
Idle-to-scratch and scratch-to-idle still switch directly; continuous stand/sit transitions are not implemented.
Full browser layout and Windows WPF compilation/runtime are not verified.
